# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: August 6, 2026*

This page describes what information this site collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. This site is a small community project, not a commercial service so this page will be relatively brief.

## Information collected when you sign in

You can sign in with an emailed link, a GitHub account, or a Google account. Depending on which you choose:

- **Email link**: we collect the email address you provide.
- **GitHub**: GitHub shares your email address (or, if you've set your GitHub email to private, a proxy address that forwards to you) and basic public profile information.
- **Google**: Google shares your email address and basic profile information (name, profile picture) as permitted by the `email` and `profile` scopes.

Sign-in and account data is managed by our authentication provider, [Supabase](https://supabase.com); we don't handle or store passwords ourselves. Your signed-in session is kept in your browser's local storage, not in a tracking cookie.

## Information you provide directly

- **Comments**: when you post a comment on a problem page, we store the display name you type (which does not have to be your real name), the comment text, the problem it's attached to, and the time it was posted. Your email address is never shown next to a comment.
- **Suggested edits and new problem proposals**: submitting a suggested edit or a new problem opens a pull request on this site's [public GitHub repository](https://github.com/pglutz/open-logic-problems). The pull request contains the content you submitted and a commit message, but not your email address in readable form. We also log a private record of each submission (which account made it, when, and a link to the resulting pull request) to prevent abuse, such as excessive submissions in a short period.

## Emails we send

We send a small number of transactional emails: sign-in links, and confirmations when a submission is opened or accepted. These are sent via [Resend](https://resend.com). We don't send newsletters or marketing email, and we don't share your email address with anyone for those purposes.

## Other service providers

Beyond the ones above, this site is hosted on [Vercel](https://vercel.com), which — like any web host — may log standard technical information (such as IP addresses) as part of normal operation. Site search is handled entirely in your browser and doesn't send your search queries anywhere.

## Analytics and advertising

We don't use analytics services, advertising trackers, or third-party tracking scripts of any kind.

## Public information

Comments and merged submissions are public, since the site and its underlying content repository are both public. Please keep that in mind when choosing what display name to use or what you write in a comment.

## Data retention and deletion

If you'd like your account or the personal information associated with it deleted, email [admin@openlogicproblems.com](mailto:admin@openlogicproblems.com) and we'll take care of it. Note that content already merged into the public repository (such as an accepted problem edit) is part of the project's public history and can't be retroactively removed, the same way a merged open-source contribution generally can't be.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above. For a project this size, we don't expect that to happen often.

## Questions

If you have questions about any of this, email [admin@openlogicproblems.com](mailto:admin@openlogicproblems.com) or open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/pglutz/open-logic-problems).
