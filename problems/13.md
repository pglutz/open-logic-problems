## Statement

The stable fields conjecture asserts that every infinite stable field is separably closed.

## Known Partial Results

- Johnson, Tran, Walsberg and Ye showed that large stable fields are separably closed [^JTW23].

## Notes

Macintyre proved that every infinite $\omega$-stable field is algebraically closed [^Mac71]. Cherlin and Shelah extended the result to infinite superstable fields [^CS80]. Wood proved that all separably closed fields are stable [^Woo79].
