## Statement

The stable fields conjecture asserts that every infinite stable field is separably closed.

## Known Partial Results

- Johnson, Tran, Walsberg and Ye showed that large stable fields are separably closed.

## Additional References

- Macintyre. *On $\omega$–categorical theories of fields.* Fundamenta Mathematicae (1971)
- Wood. *Notes on the stability of separably closed fields*. Journal of Symbolic Logic (1979)
- Cherlin and Shelah. *Superstable fields and groups.* Annals of Mathematical Logic (1980)
- Johnson,Tran, Walsberg, and Ye. *The Étale-open topology and the stable fields conjecture.* Journal of the European mathematical society (2023)

## Notes

Macintyre proved that every infinite $\omega$-stable field is algebraically closed. Cherlin and Shelah extended the result to infinite superstable fields. Wood proved that all separably closed fields are stable.
