## Statement

The stable fields conjecture asserts that every infinite stable field is separably closed.

## Known Partial Results

- Johnson, Tran, Walsberg and Ye showed that large stable fields are separably closed [^johnson-tran-walsberg-ye2023].

## Notes

Macintyre proved that every infinite $\omega$-stable field is algebraically closed [^macintyre1971]. Cherlin and Shelah extended the result to infinite superstable fields [^cherlin-shelah1980]. Wood proved that all separably closed fields are stable [^wood1979].
