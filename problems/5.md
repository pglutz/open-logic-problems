## Statement

The **Partition Principle** ($\mathsf{PP}$) states that for any sets $A$ and $B$, if there is a surjection from $A$ to $B$ then there is an injection from $B$ to $A$. This question asks whether the Partition Principle implies the Axiom of Choice over $\mathsf{ZF}$.

## Known Partial Results

- Pincus proved that $\mathsf{PP}$ implies that every well-ordered family of nonempty sets has a choice function.
- By a result of Jensen, the above theorem of Pincus shows that $\mathsf{PP}$ also implies $\mathsf{DC}$, the Axiom of Dependent Choice.

## Notes

This question was first posed in the early 1900s and is one of the oldest open problems in set theory.
